Security design
1. Default-off temporary feature, production-project and company pinning.
2. Fresh approved Super Admin identity on every action, plus fresh database
   company membership/module checks. Ordinary Admin/Management cannot operate it.
3. Same-origin production Server Actions; no arbitrary URLs, headers, recipients,
   company or actor supplied by the browser.
4. New private callback_setup ledger, forced RLS, no direct anon/authenticated/
   service-role access. Existing NOLOGIN executor receives only SELECT and six
   specific UPDATE columns. Runtime cannot create, renew or reset a permit.
5. Operator-issued permit lasts at most 15 minutes; confirmation lasts at most
   two minutes and binds actor, operation, deployment and before/after hashes.
6. Atomic row-lock consumption before PATCH; one concurrent winner. Expired,
   revoked, used and uncertain operations do not reopen. Fresh post-lock checks.
7. Only bounded HTTPS health(GET,wakeup=false), settings GET and one settings
   PATCH. No redirect following, raw response/error logging or secret responses.
8. Read current settings twice across preview/confirmation, compare fingerprints,
   preserve all fields, inspect PATCH before/after and independently GET verify.
   Unknown/masked/schema-incompatible or incomplete snapshots stop setup rather
   than being dropped or filled with invented defaults. Channel must be AUTH;
   equivalent callback URL aliases are rejected before adding subscriptions.
9. Exact already-configured callback is a no-op (GET only) but consumes the permit.
10. Uncertain update consumes the permit permanently. Read-only recovery compares
    saved settings with the reserved digest without retrying or rewriting history.

Recovery / limitations
- Whapi documentation is inconsistent about partial PATCH omission semantics.
  This installer always preserves/replays the full validated settings object.
- No documented ETag/conditional update is available. Double-read hashes detect
  drift but cannot prevent another Whapi operator editing between GET and PATCH.
  The exclusive settings window is necessary; do not claim atomic cross-system
  settings preservation. Never automatically revert another operator's change.
- Strict schema validation may safely block an older/newer provider schema.
  Review actual key/type differences privately, never log settings/headers.
- Configuration read-back does not establish real callback delivery or approval
  for tenant messaging. Provider capacity/content, genuine consent, Rosley
  ownership, legal/access-safety and sender handover requirements remain separate.
- If an attempted PATCH or outcome write is uncertain, retain the consumed ledger,
  keep sends off, inspect GET only and investigate. Do not blindly retry.
- If a permit expires unused, an operator must review the reason; no application
  reissue/reset exists. Revocation is the immediate stop for unused deployments.
- No automatic self-deletion is claimed: cleanup requires the reviewed follow-up
  removal deployment plus RPC revocation, not just changing an environment value.
