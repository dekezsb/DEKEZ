# Monthly Tenant Invoice Generation SOP

## Purpose

Ensure every active tenant can see the correct monthly rental invoice in the tenant portal before payment is due.

## Standard rule

1. Generate and show each tenant's new monthly rental invoice exactly seven calendar days before that tenant's own due date.
2. Apply this rule every month to every active tenant across all properties.
3. Use each tenant's individual due day. Do not use one common release date for all tenants.
4. The invoice must be labelled for the rental month it belongs to and must not be carried forward or matched as another month's rent.
5. Do not generate a new monthly invoice after the tenant has checked out or billing has been formally terminated.
6. Run the automatic billing check daily at 12:05 a.m. Malaysia time so invoices become available on the correct date.

## Examples

- Rent due on 1 September: show the September invoice on 25 August.
- Rent due on 5 September: show the September invoice on 29 August.
- Rent due on 7 September: show the September invoice on 31 August.

## Monthly control check

Before the new rental month starts, confirm:

- Every active tenant whose seven-day window has opened has a visible invoice in the tenant portal.
- The invoice rental month and due date are correct.
- Checked-out tenants have not received a new invoice.
- No active tenant has duplicate invoices for the same rental month.

## Exception handling

Any waiver, rent adjustment, extra charge, electricity top-up, or manual billing change must be recorded separately with its reason and must not silently change this seven-day invoice-generation rule.
