Purpose and scope
-----------------
Extend the existing rental-reminder workflow, not accounting or tenancy enforcement.
The original invoice due date is the ONLY aging anchor. Verified partial payments
reduce the balance; they do not extend or restart the clock. The policy stages are:
-7 advance; 0 due; 1–6 daily; 7 urgent account review; 8–13 daily final warning;
14 formal tenancy termination review; 15+ daily checks and urgent follow-up.
No weekly fallback. No physical access, utility, eviction or termination action.

Sources and accounting boundaries
--------------------------------
Company-scoped rent_bills and properties; authoritative non-deposit invoice amount
plus rental_invoice_line_items less recorded paid_amount. Confirmed, non-reversed
payment allocations corroborate that recorded paid amount, never subtract twice.
Parent payment submissions and bank matches are not added as second payments.
Pending verification is not settlement. Security deposits are excluded.
Invoice extras are identified in message breakdowns. Future invoices are scanned
but not demanded as arrears. Invoices due exactly seven days later receive advance
reminders only when actually issued and otherwise valid.

All company invoice records remain visible for audit. Cancelled/draft/waived,
internal, unissued, inactive, unlinked, or unreconciled balances are exceptions,
not verified default claims and not formal termination-review notices. Recorded
balance and verified collectible balance are distinct. Zero recorded balance with
unresolved financial evidence is not reported as verified settlement.
Pending proof, disputes and messaging holds do not reset an otherwise valid
invoice clock. Cases stay in the review ledger; payment demands are withheld.
