Standing automation policy — no daily approval
---------------------------------------------
After initial production/policy activation, the authenticated 09:00 Malaysia cron
runs unattended every day. There is NO daily approval token, batch-signoff screen
or per-message Management approval requirement. The initial rollout preview and
approval below are initial activation checks, not a recurring operational gate.
The same standing policy covers newly eligible tenants after their genuine
number-bound ownership/consent and source exceptions have been verified through
authorized existing controls. Do not request another daily batch approval.

Each day scans all relevant invoices and persists case/review history, recomputes
verified balances and original invoice aging, then independently rechecks every
claim. A tenant-specific consent/STOP, phone, payment, dispute, communication or
source hold skips that tenant and remains in the Management worklist; other
eligible tenants continue, including on later pages. Verified partial payments
reduce only the outstanding amount, never reset the original due date. Day7 and
Day14 internal review tasks remain mandatory; only humans may authorize legally
significant restrictions, notices or termination decisions. No physical access or
essential-service restriction is authorized by a reminder or review task.
An unrenderable/oversized reminder records a source-fingerprint-bound Management
follow-up without claiming a send, then continues with other eligible tenants.
Repeated scans preserve that hold for the unchanged source. Failure to persist
the follow-up is a global safety incident, not a silent skip.

Human approval is retained for initial activation/policy, sensitive rule changes,
genuine identity/consent/payment exceptions, Day7/14 decisions and legal notices
or enforcement. A global database/provider/security failure, revoked company
clearance, rate limit, uncertain send or exhausted runtime budget must still fail
closed. This is an operational incident, not a routine daily approval requirement.
Do not bypass unknown deliveries to claim uninterrupted service. Capacity/latency
and monitoring must be cleared before promising the unattended production rollout.

Purpose, stages and financial boundaries
---------------------------------------
This is a first-party rental payment-reminder and internal-review workflow, not
automatic enforcement. Original invoice due date is the ONLY aging anchor.
Verified partial payment reduces balance and never resets the overdue clock.
Stages: -7 advance; 0 due; 1–6 daily; 7 urgent internal account review; 8–13 daily;
14 formal tenancy-review referral; 15+ daily tracking and lawful follow-up.
Day 7/14 are policy review thresholds, not legal findings or statutory entitlements.
No automatic physical lockout, access-card/fingerprint suspension, utility cut-off,
tenancy termination, eviction, tenancy-date change or financial adjustment.

Company-scoped rent_bills and properties are the source. Non-deposit invoice amount
plus rental_invoice_line_items less recorded paid_amount is calculated once.
Verified, non-reversed allocations corroborate paid_amount, never subtract twice.
Parent payment submissions and bank matches are not additional payments.
Deposits are excluded. Extras are identified in message breakdowns. Future invoices
are scanned but not demanded as arrears; the 7-day reminder requires a valid issued
invoice. Fully verified settlement ends reminders. Pending proof is not settlement:
it imposes a database send hold and Admin follow-up; template H is preview-only.

Cancelled/draft/waived/internal/unissued, inactive/checked-out, unlinked, identity
mismatches and unreconciled balances remain visible as source exceptions. They are
not formal default claims. Zero recorded balance with unresolved payment evidence
is not verified settlement. Pending proof, disputes and contact holds retain the
original financial aging while withholding payment demands. Only valid collectible
invoices trigger internal threshold reviews. Source exceptions cannot be repaired
through this communications module.
