DEKEZ RENTAL COLLECTION POLICY — DRAFT / NO PRODUCTION ACTIVATION
27 October 2026

Purpose and scope
-----------------
Extend the existing rental-reminder workflow, not accounting or tenancy enforcement.
The original invoice due date is the ONLY aging anchor. Verified partial payments
reduce the balance; they do not extend or restart the clock. The policy stages are:
-7 advance; 0 due; 1–6 daily; 7 urgent account review; 8–13 daily final warning;
14 formal tenancy termination review; 15+ daily checks and urgent follow-up.
No weekly fallback. No physical access, utility, eviction or termination action.

Sources and accounting boundaries
--------------------------------
Company-scoped rent_bills and properties; authoritative non-deposit invoice amount
plus rental_invoice_line_items less recorded paid_amount. Confirmed, non-reversed
payment allocations corroborate that recorded paid amount, never subtract twice.
Parent payment submissions and bank matches are not added as second payments.
Pending verification is not settlement. Security deposits are excluded.
Invoice extras are identified in message breakdowns. Future invoices are scanned
but not demanded as arrears. Invoices due exactly seven days later receive advance
reminders only when actually issued and otherwise valid.

All company invoice records remain visible for audit. Cancelled/draft/waived,
internal, unissued, inactive, unlinked, or unreconciled balances are exceptions,
not verified default claims and not formal termination-review notices. Recorded
balance and verified collectible balance are distinct. Zero recorded balance with
unresolved financial evidence is not reported as verified settlement.
Pending proof, disputes and messaging holds do not reset an otherwise valid
invoice clock. Cases stay in the review ledger; payment demands are withheld.

Integration
-----------
Admin: /admin-setup/rental-reminders (fresh reportAdministrator authorization).
Read-only preview/history, existing Admin design, 25 groups per page. No send or
activation controls. Portal roles cannot call the service-only reporting RPCs.

The full company scan/review transaction completes BEFORE delivery processing.
Internal Day 7/14 reviews are recorded once per invoice/threshold. Public claim
also records review atomically so outgoing text never claims a nonexistent review.

  DEKEZ_COLLECTION_SEND_ENABLED=false

No arbitrary recipient parameter: current verified tenant identity, exact-number
consent and private policy gate determine the destination.
Existing personal management-report recipient and command allowlist are untouched.

Proposed schedule only: config/rental-collection-cron.pending.json.
After separate approval, append /api/rental-collections at 0 1 * * * UTC (09:00 MYT).

Legacy handover and access safety
--------------------------------
When POLICY_ENABLED is true, the existing hourly /api/rent-reminders stops its
legacy Meta rent query/sending, while retaining enrollment synchronization.
Same-day legacy reminder logs suppress new-engine sends during handover.
Activation must drain old in-flight deployments/workers before enabling the new
sender; otherwise an old worker could race after its initial query.
Manual Meta messaging is unchanged, not a second scheduled collection engine.
The draft removes the hourly suspendOverdueFingerprintAccess invocation because
arrears must not automatically lock tenants out. It does not change lock records.
SEPARATE PRE-EXISTING RISK: payment-linked 30-day fingerprint validity and already
configured device expiries still exist. Do not claim residential access is fully
protected until those are independently reviewed and lawfully remediated.

Delivery semantics
------------------
Fresh source/fingerprint recheck after lock waits; permanent unique tenant/day
and recipient/day claims. Original due dates are never written. Pending proof
is an unconditional database send hold (Admin review; template H is preview only).
The existing fixed-origin Whapi client is reused, without redirects or retries.
Sequential sends with one-second pacing between accepted messages; stop on any
rejection/uncertainty, including 429. Confirm channel-specific limits before use.
Provider accepted is NOT proof of delivered/read. Record provider ID/status safely.
Interrupted sending claims are uncertain and must never be reset blindly.
Supabase and Whapi cannot share a transaction. A payment/opt-out committed AFTER
the final claim can race the external request; no impossible exactly-once delivery
or instant cross-system cancellation guarantee is made.
The worker has a 300-second budget and stops explicitly as incomplete with reserved
finish time. Full internal scan is already recorded; unsent cases remain visible.
Benchmark eligible volume/provider latency before activation; add a reviewed durable
continuation worker if needed. No self-triggering/retry workaround is enabled here.
