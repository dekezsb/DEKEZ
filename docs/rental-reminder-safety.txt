DEKEZ rental payment reminder safety review
Review scope: PR #32, local changes only. No production activation.

1. Ordinary reminders versus enforcement

Ordinary invoice-specific payment reminders are not automatically prohibited
debt collection. WhatsApp's official utility guidance expressly includes payment
reminders and account updates for existing transactions or subscriptions:
https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/conversation-categories/utility/

The Business Messaging Policy separately lists debt collection, requires opt-in,
requires on/off-platform opt-outs to be respected and prohibits spam or undue
pressure. It does not provide a precise first-party rental/third-party collection
boundary, or say that every overdue invoice reminder is prohibited:
https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/

Neutral amount/due-date/payment-status reminders can be reviewed as ordinary
account notifications. The original repeated final-warning, restriction and
termination wording is different. Its exact content and frequency need explicit
business/provider review; actual notice or enforcement claims need review of the
signed tenancy agreement and applicable law. Do not substitute generic Day 7/14
thresholds for an actual contractual cure period or legal process. Internal
review stages do not themselves issue a termination, eviction or access order.

Whapi is an independent, unofficial linked-device tool; its provider acceptance
does not establish Meta policy approval or proof of recipient delivery:
https://whapi.cloud/terms
https://whapi.cloud/docs

No token, channel configuration or provider request was used for this review.

2. Legacy reminder handover

The revised /api/rent-reminders route permanently stops its legacy scheduled
Meta rent messages. It preserves the same hourly cron and its existing pending
fingerprint enrollment reconciliation. The retired rent sender cannot resume
because new-engine flags are absent, false, malformed, rolled back in settings,
or because someone adds query parameters. The route no longer queries invoices,
creates WhatsApp threads or writes reminder/message records.

This is a local code guarantee, not a claim about older immutable deployments.
Before separately approving the new sender, deploy the reviewed retirement,
disable/restrict any old deployment URLs able to run the sender, stop manual old
invocations, and allow in-flight old requests to finish. Inspect their final
logs and reconcile same-day/uncertain sends before approving the handover in the
new engine. Never enable two senders during a rolling change. Do not rotate or
weaken cron authentication merely to skip this review. The seven existing cron
entries and the 20:00 Malaysia management report remain unchanged.

On rollback, keep new sending disabled and do not roll production back to a
version that re-enables the legacy sender. Preserve private attempt history.
An accepted/uncertain/crashed provider call must not be blindly retried.

3. Physical-access audit and remaining blocker

The legacy cron no longer calls suspendOverdueFingerprintAccess. Repository
search found no remaining legitimate caller of that helper. Its implementation
is now a compatibility no-op: no invoice query, credential shortening, device
call, database mutation or tenant message. Enrollment and Smart Devices copy no
longer promise automatic Day 7 physical-access suspension.

This does NOT mean the existing device-expiry risk is cleared. In the unchanged
lib/ttlock/fingerprint.ts, accessPeriod provisions approximately 30 days capped
by the current contract end. Existing payment-verification/rent-due workflows
call extendFingerprintAccessAfterPayment. An existing credential can therefore
expire without any reminder or explicit suspension call. Passcode provisioning
in lib/ttlock/access.ts uses the tenancy end, or a 30-day period when a monthly
stay lacks an end date. Those legitimate provisioning, payment-extension and
checkout/revocation paths were NOT altered by this reminder change.

Before activation, Management must commission a read-only inventory of actual
device and database validity dates for current occupiers, identify any grants
whose continuation depends on rent payment, and approve a separate lawful access
continuity policy with security/legal review. Any required device-period changes
need separate authority, testing and audit. No physical access was extended,
revoked or restored in this work; no tenant/payment/tenancy date was changed.
Do not represent passive expiry as a lawful unpaid-rent enforcement method.

Preserve documented checkout, lost/stolen credentials and legitimate security
revocation. Do not automatically extend a checked-out or unauthorized occupant.
The old 30-day/payment coupling remains an explicit activation blocker until
reviewed, not something hidden by disabling the suspension helper.

4. Sending safeguards that must stay in force

Use the canonical active tenant's current portal number as an address source,
not proof of WhatsApp ownership or consent. Keep recorded exact-number ownership,
opt-in, opt-out/STOP ingestion, holds/disputes and fresh verified balance checks.
Never route a tenant demand to the sender or management digest recipients.
Pending payment proof requires neutral verification handling, not another demand.
Preserve the original invoice due date after partial payment; reduce only the
verified outstanding amount. Consolidate to at most one reminder per tenant and
phone per Malaysia date. Keep permanent atomic attempt reservations and clear
provider accepted/delivered/read/failed/uncertain distinctions.

Capacity approval must use measured database/provider latency and channel-specific
approved pacing, not a universal assumption that one message per second is safe.
If a batch cannot fit its reviewed function/window and budget, fail closed before
additional sends, expose incomplete/held cases to Management and require a fresh
review. Do not silently drop cases, spawn unbounded sends or automatically retry
unknown results. A database commit and external HTTP send cannot be one atomic
transaction; fresh pre-send checks reduce but cannot eliminate that boundary.

5. Verification scope

tests/rental-collection-legacy.test.cjs covers authenticated no-send behavior for
all new-engine flag states, query overrides and repeated requests, enrollment
error redaction, and preservation of seven cron entries.
tests/rental-access-safety.test.cjs executes the dormant helper with forbidden
database/device/messaging mocks and verifies no calls occur. Regression snapshots
guard the unchanged access-period, payment-extension, passcode and checkout code.
Native synthetic PostgreSQL tests and the full release suite are separate gates;
their latest executed results must be reported, not inferred from this document.

Production migrations, release, settings, schedule activation, tenant messages,
provider configuration and device access changes still require separate approval.
